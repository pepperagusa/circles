How to play

Rosette is a daily puzzle inspired by puzzle cubes. Six groups of four coloured dots sit at the intersections of six interlocking rings.

Drag along any ring to rotate its dots. Each move shifts a ring by one step, scrambling colours across neighbouring rings.

Your goal: return every colour to its original cluster. A new puzzle appears each day — same scramble for everyone.
